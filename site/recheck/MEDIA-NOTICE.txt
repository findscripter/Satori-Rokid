Changes for Satori-Rokid (2026-11-23)
- sequence-01-4k.mp4 is derived from Scrambled Eggs / GoPro recording 16_40.
- sequence-02-4k.mp4 is derived from Scrambled Eggs / GoPro recording 16_3.
- Both original 3840x2160 HEVC video streams were transcoded to browser-friendly
  H.264. The video frame order and presentation timestamps were preserved. Audio,
  other tracks, and original container metadata were omitted from these derivatives.
- The posters in media/ and the 62 images in frames/ are resized JPEG extracts
  from the corresponding lower-resolution video streams of these two recordings.
- The interactive overlays, model decisions, additional visual assessments,
  charts, and timing statistics were produced for this Satori-Rokid experiment.
  They are not annotations endorsed by the CaptainCook4D authors.
